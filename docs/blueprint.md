# **App Name**: PrepAmerica

## Core Features:

- Personalized Preparedness Planner: An interactive tool that takes user input (family size, location, local risks) to generate a customized emergency preparedness plan, including recommended supplies and actions, and linking to relevant resources and products.
- Comprehensive Resource Library: A collection of static educational content, articles, and downloadable guides covering various aspects of emergency preparedness.
- Online Training Courses & Certifications: Display a catalog of emergency preparedness courses, allowing users to browse course details and enroll to work towards certifications.
- Curated Affiliate Store: An e-commerce section displaying emergency preparedness products categorized (e.g., Water, Food, Power) with direct Amazon affiliate links.
- Gear Reviews & Buying Guides: Feature detailed reviews, comparisons, and buying guides for essential emergency preparedness equipment.
- Newsletter & 72-Hour Checklist Sign-up: Implement a subscription form for the PrepAmerica newsletter, offering a free downloadable 72-hour emergency checklist as an incentive.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep, professional blue (#336699) conveying trust and reliability, contrasting well with the light background.
- Background color: A very light, subtly tinted blue-grey (#F2F7FB) for a clean and accessible feel, maintaining readability for extensive content.
- Accent color: A vibrant orange (#d58936) for calls to action, important links, and highlighting key information to draw user attention.
- Headline and body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its modern, neutral, and highly readable qualities, suitable for educational content.
- Use clear, informative, and subtly stylized icons that enhance understanding of preparedness concepts without being overly playful. Opt for solid or outline icons that fit a professional aesthetic.
- Employ a clean, structured, and modular layout with ample whitespace to ensure easy navigation and digestibility of information. Prioritize content hierarchy and responsive design for all devices.
